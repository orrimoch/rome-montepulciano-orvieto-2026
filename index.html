Chiusi is the change point. Rome → Montepulciano: train to Chiusi, then weekday FT4 bus. Montepulciano → Orvieto: weekday FT4 back to Chiusi, then train south to Orvieto. You do not need to pass through Terni. The FT4 PDF valid from 5 October lists the weekday trips used here; T4B is for Sundays and holidays. Allow for the uphill walk between Montepulciano’s bus station and old town.
